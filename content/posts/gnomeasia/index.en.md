## Day 0
Day 0 was the trip to Malaysia, and it was a first for me: my first time traveling solo to another country, and Malaysia was the trial run.
My Air Asia flight left from Soekarno-Hatta International Airport for Kuala Lumpur.
![In the Air Asia cabin](assets/day0-1.webp "In the Air Asia cabin")
I landed at Kuala Lumpur International Airport at 2 AM.
![Breakfast in KL](assets/breakfast_my.webp "Breakfast after arriving in KL")
I took a Grab car from the airport to my [hotel](https://goo.gl/maps/R6gA3KfagmZ5G64o7). Once I got to the [hotel](https://goo.gl/maps/R6gA3KfagmZ5G64o7), I rested up for the summit.

## Day 1
On day 1, I took a Grab car from the hotel to the [venue](https://goo.gl/maps/P25geXMorwDCXyxk7).
![GNOME ASIA](assets/day_1_gnome.webp "GNOME.Asia banner")
I met some people I'd only known from Twitter, like Kristi, Anisa Kuci, and others.
Day 1 had lots of great speakers. I was especially interested in Kristi's and Anisa Kuci's talks about GNOME and community, because I love contributing to the open source community.
![Chatting with Anisa](assets/meetanisa.webp "Talking with Anisa")
![Anisa](assets/day1-annisa.webp "Anisa talking about community")
![Kristi](assets/kristi.webp "Kristi talking about GNOME")
In the afternoon we had lunch at a market to try local Malaysian food. To me, Indonesian and Malaysian food are pretty much the same.
![Lunch on day 1](assets/lunch-gnome-asia.webp "Lunch on day 1 of GNOME.Asia")

After lunch we tried soya, a Malaysian specialty.
![Trying soya](assets/fresh-soya.webp "Trying soya")
After day 1, I walked back to my hotel.
![After day 1](assets/after-day1.webp "Walking back to the hotel")

## Day 2
Day 2 was a special day for me. Why? Because my talk was on day 2. And for my talk I brought my keyboard to show off :p
![My ergo keyboard](assets/mykeyboard.webp "My ergo keyboard")
In my talk, I shared my journey with [GNOME](https://gnome.org), [openSUSE](https://opensuse.org), and my mechanical [keyboard](https://mechanicalkeyboards.co.id/).
Some people were interested in my talk, and a few tried my keyboard, like [Matthias](https://twitter.com/matthias_clasen).
![People trying my keyboard](assets/mykey.webp "Matthias trying my keyboard")
![My talk about my journey with open source](assets/flexing.webp "My talk")

After my talk, we had lunch at a laid-back restaurant in Kuala Lumpur, and I tried nasi ambeng.
![Nasi ambeng](assets/lunch-day2.webp "Nasi ambeng")

At this year's GNOME.Asia, lots of students who joined GSoC presented their projects, and it was awesome.
![Pooja](assets/poojaday2.webp "Pooja talking about her GSoC project")

The final talk was Syazwan sharing his journey with open source.
![Syazwan](assets/syazwan.webp "Syazwan talking about his journey with open source")
