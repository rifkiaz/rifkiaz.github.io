This is a list of every Beyblade X part that's been released or announced, from Basic Line (BX) and Unique Line (UX) to Custom Line (CX). Ratchets and bits share one list because they work with any line. If you want to know which ones are winning a lot right now, see [The Current Beyblade X Meta](/en/hobi/beyblade-x-meta/). Wondering about thick and thin bits? See [this section](#thick-bits-vs-thin-bits).

The code under each name is the product the part first appeared in. BX-00, UX-00, or CX-00 means a special release (an event, a prize, or a limited set), and codes starting with G are Hasbro releases.

All images are official Takara Tomy or Hasbro renders I took from the [Beyblade Wiki](https://beyblade.fandom.com/) on Fandom. The copyright stays with them, so every image has a "Source" link to its original page. Data collected November 2, 2026.

## Ratchets

The first number is the number of protrusions, the second is the height in tenths of a millimeter. Ratchets starting with M have a metal ring at the bottom, so they're heavier.

## Thick bits vs thin bits

When people talk about "thick" and "thin" bits, they don't mean the tip that touches the stadium. They mean the **shaft**: the post at the top of the bit that goes into the ratchet. How thick that shaft is decides the bit's **Burst Resistance** stat. In Beyblade X there are only two values: **80** for a thick shaft and **30** for a thin one.

### Why does the shaft affect bursting?

A ratchet locks onto the blade by twisting. Inside the ratchet there's a lock that can flex. Once you attach a bit, its shaft fills the hole in the ratchet and props that lock up from the inside.

- A **thick shaft** fills the hole and pushes the lock outward, so the lock can barely flex. The blade has a harder time twisting loose when it takes a hit, so it bursts less often.
- A **thin shaft** leaves a gap. The lock has room to flex, so your combo comes apart more easily against hard hits.

{{< beyblade-poros "diagram" >}}

### Which bits are thick and which are thin?

The pattern is easy to remember: almost every **Attack** and **Balance** bit has a thick shaft, while **Defense** and **Stamina** bits have thin ones. The exception worth knowing is **Elevate**: it's a Balance bit, but its shaft is thin (Burst Resistance 30).

{{< beyblade-poros "daftar" >}}

This list is based on the official Burst Resistance stats listed on the Beyblade Wiki as of November 20, 2026. Bits built into a ratchet (Operate and Turbo) aren't included, since their shaft isn't a separate part.

### So are thin bits worse?

Not really. Most thin bits are stamina and defense bits, and their shafts are slim by design. Ball, Free Ball, and Elevate still win plenty in the [current meta](/en/hobi/beyblade-x-meta/). The difference is risk: a combo on a thin bit bursts more easily against a strong attacker, so pick a sturdy blade and ratchet to make up for it.

A few more things worth knowing:

- **Simple-type ratchets** (Hasbro releases with an "O" joint, which Hasbro calls RBT-Ratchets) hold Burst Resistance at 30 for every bit, so a thick shaft gives no advantage on them.
- For the **M-85** ratchet, some community guides say thick and thin bits end up equally strong because it uses a newer lock design. That comes from community testing, not official data.
- Bursting still depends on the blade, ratchet, and bit together. Tall ratchets (like 80) are also easier to hit from below, so don't look at the shaft alone.

### Don't mix it up with bit height

Some people also call Low or Under bits "thin" because they're shorter. That's about **height**, not the shaft, and it does something different:

| Series | Height vs. a standard bit | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Under | 2 mm shorter | Under Flat, Under Needle |
| Low | 1 mm shorter | Low Flat, Low Rush, Low Orb, Low Point |
| Standard | baseline | Flat, Rush, Needle, Ball |
| High | 1 mm taller | High Needle, High Taper |

A shorter bit lowers the blade's contact point, which makes upper attacks easier. A taller bit does the opposite. Height doesn't change the shaft: Low Rush still has a thick shaft, and Under Needle still has a thin one.
